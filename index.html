Locus · Study & Progress Privacy Policy Last updated: October 23, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how Locus: Study & Progress ("Locus", "we", "our") handles information when you use our mobile application. If you have questions, contact aqvstudios@gmail.com. At a glance Locus can be used without creating an account. Your focus history, notes, and related content are designed to be stored locally on your device. However, third-party services used for advertising and purchases may process information as described below. 1. Information you provide and store Depending on the features you use, Locus may store focus sessions, statistics, goals, preferences, tags, moods, notes, and attachments such as photos or audio recordings on your device. We do not operate a Locus account system or a cloud-sync service for this information in the current version. Content stored locally may be affected by device loss, app deletion, operating-system behavior, or device backups. If your device's backup service is enabled, your operating-system provider may process backed-up app data under its own terms and settings. 2. Permissions and device features Camera and photos: Used when you choose to capture or attach images to notes or save sharing cards. Microphone/audio: Used if you choose to create audio attachments, where supported. Notifications: Used for reminders, focus-session updates, and other notifications you enable. Background operation and alarms: Certain device capabilities may be used to support focus timers and reminders. Permissions are requested where applicable. You can manage many permissions in your device settings. Exact permission behavior can vary by Android version. 3. Advertising Locus uses Google AdMob to display advertisements in the free version. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, device information, and ad interactions, subject to the applicable configuration, user choices, and law. Advertising-related data may be used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, personalization. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's guidance for AdMob publishers. 4. Premium purchases If you purchase a Premium subscription, billing and payment processing are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Purchase status and subscription-related information may be made available to the app or its purchase-processing components to enable Premium features and restore eligible purchases. 5. How information is used Locally stored information supports the features you request, including focus tracking, statistics, notes, customization, and sharing cards. Information processed by third-party services is used for their respective purposes, such as advertising and purchase processing, as described in their policies. 6. Sharing and third-party services We do not provide an in-app public profile or cloud-based social feed in the current version. When you choose to share a session card through another app, the information included in that card is shared through the destination service you select. Google AdMob and Google Play may process data independently as necessary to provide their services. 7. Retention and deletion Locally stored content generally remains on your device until you remove it through available app controls, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Removing the app may not delete copies you previously exported, shared, or backed up using other services. For requests or questions concerning data handled by Locus, contact us at aqvstudios@gmail.com. For data handled directly by Google, consult Google's privacy controls and policies. 8. Security We aim to limit unnecessary access to your information. However, no device, application, or transmission method is completely secure. Protect access to your device and use its available security features. 9. Children's privacy Locus is a general productivity application. We do not intentionally operate an account system for collecting children's personal information. The applicable age audience, advertising configuration, and any additional protections will follow the app's Google Play declarations and relevant legal requirements. Parents or guardians with questions can contact us at the address below. 10. International use Locus may be used in multiple countries. Third-party providers, including Google, may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their privacy policies and applicable safeguards. 11. Changes to this policy We may update this policy as Locus features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date. 12.
